Privacy Policy for Unit Price

Last updated: 2026-10-08

This policy describes Unit Price (com.myhome.unitprice).

Information stored by the app

Saved stores, product categories, offer amounts, and unit-price comparisons. Records and settings are stored in app storage on your device. These apps do not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization. Android device backup behavior may depend on your system settings.

Photos

Where photo features are provided, photos you select are stored with the app's local data and used for the related records. They are not uploaded by this app to a developer-operated cloud service.

Advertising

The free version includes Google AdMob. This closed-test build uses Google's test ad units rather than live advertising. Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform may process device, consent, and diagnostic information under Google's privacy terms. A production release may use live ad units after its release configuration and disclosures are reviewed.

Purchases

An optional Pro in-app product may be offered through Google Play. Google Play processes purchase and payment details; this app does not receive your full payment card number.

Analytics

Portfolio analytics is disabled in this closed-test build. A production release may offer optional Google Analytics for Firebase after consent. If enabled, analytics is limited to app lifecycle, purchase, and product-health events and does not include names, notes, dates, photos, categories, locations, or other record contents entered by the user.

Sharing

The app does not provide an account or cloud-sync service. Information you choose to share or export is sent through Android's selected share destination and is then handled by that destination.

Data retention and deletion

Records remain in app storage until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its data. Any information exported or shared to another app is subject to that app's own retention and privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its SDK configuration, or its data practices change. The current version remains available at this address.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact 1000usefulapps@gmail.com.
